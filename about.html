The person behind the work

I’m a website developer and AI automation engineer, currently studying artificial intelligence with a minor in cybersecurity.

I enjoy building for the web because good software can make a complicated process feel clear. I’m also exploring how AI and automation can take care of repetitive steps, connect useful information and help people make better use of their time.

My approach starts with listening: understand the people and problem first, then choose the simplest technology that can do the job well. I value readable interfaces, thoughtful details, dependable behavior and honest communication about what a product can do.

I’m also certified in web and app development, AI and machine learning engineering, software engineering, and data analysis.
